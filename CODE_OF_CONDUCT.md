# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want participation in cowbox to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise when discussing code and design.
- Critique ideas and behavior, not people.
- Assume good intent, but ask for evidence when claims affect security or data.
- Respect privacy: do not paste secrets, private source, or personal data into
  issues, pull requests, or benchmark fixtures.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope and milestone boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, hidden fallbacks, or fabricated test
  results.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after a decision has been made.

## Enforcement

Report serious incidents to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting
or the contact listed on the repository profile. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
contributors. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This is a small M1 project; enforcement decisions are made by the maintainer,
with a bias toward restoring a productive technical discussion.
